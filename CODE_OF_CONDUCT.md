# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want a space where people can disagree about technical decisions without being
made to feel unwelcome. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique the rule, the evidence or the code — not the person.
- Assume the other person has context you do not. Ask before concluding.
- Be explicit about uncertainty. "I don't know" is a valid contribution.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's real data, names, paths or messages.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Deliberately weakening a safety/verification check to win an argument.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers listed in the repository profile. Maintainers
may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors. Reports are handled
confidentially.
